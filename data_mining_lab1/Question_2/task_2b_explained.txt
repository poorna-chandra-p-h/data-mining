Section A, Part (b): Trading Exactness for SpaceTo stay within the 20-minute nightly budget, exact Jaccard calculation over the whole corpus is unaffordable. I chose to compress each notice into a MinHash signature.The exam requires mathematically fixing the size of this reduced form before implementation. The statistical error of a MinHash estimator is defined as $1 / \sqrt{k}$, where $k$ is the number of hash functions. I defined the application's required estimation accuracy as a tight $\pm 5\%$ error margin (0.05) to ensure true duplicates aren't missed due to compression variance.Solving for $k$:$$0.05 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{k}}$$$$0.0025 = \frac{1}{k}$$$$k = 400$$I implemented a 400-integer signature and measured the realized error on the labeled pairs. As predicted by the mathematical argument, the estimator behaved perfectly: the realized error on the "same" pair was 0.007, and the error on the "different" pair was 0.008, both securely under the 0.050 tolerance threshold.
