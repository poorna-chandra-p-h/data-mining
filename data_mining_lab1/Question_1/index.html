Task D

Ask for March. Get March’s price.

The query stays the same. Only the reporting period parameter changes; price validity is resolved against the sale’s business date.

One query, two periods

SCD2 join
JOIN pg.price_revisions pr
  ON f.product_sk = pr.product_sk
 AND f.business_date BETWEEN
     pr.effective_from AND pr.effective_to
WHERE f.sales_month = ?
